Research

* indicates equal contribution, † indicates corresponding author

My research interests lie broadly in computer vision, 3D vision, and robotics. I have worked on diffusion-based data augmentation, robot perception, and locomotion, exploring problems across both visual understanding and robotic systems. I am currently interested in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and how visual perception and multimodal understanding can be connected to robotic intelligence. I enjoy exploring different research areas, trying new problems, and discovering new connections across fields.
